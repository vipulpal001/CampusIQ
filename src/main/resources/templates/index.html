Assistance & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about accessing and navigating CampusIQ.

Click the "Login" button on the top right. You can enter your Institutional Username, Registered Email, Student Roll Number (e.g. 2024CSE042), or Faculty Employee Code. Default starter accounts (student/student123 and faculty/faculty123) can also be tested using the one-click quick fill chips.
When creating a new account via the Register page, choose whether you are a Student, Faculty, or Administrator. CampusIQ immediately delivers a 6-digit one-time password (OTP) to your email. Submitting the OTP verifies your identity and automatically provisions your SIS profile.
As per university guidelines, all students must maintain at least 75% attendance across each registered subject. The system visually highlights safe attendance in green and flags any shortages in red on both student and faculty dashboards.
To register an Administrator profile, switch to the "Admin" pill on the register page and provide the institutional passkey (ADMIN123). Admin accounts receive full permissions to manage students, courses, notices, and timetables.
